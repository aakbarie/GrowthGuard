## Growth Chart Standards

### CDC Growth Charts (2000)
Based on NHANES data representing a diverse US population. Used for children 2–20 years.

### WHO Growth Standards (2006)
International standards based on optimal growth conditions; represent how children should grow when basic health needs are met.

## Quality Assurance

- Data validated against original CDC/WHO publications
- Calculation methods verified by pediatric professionals
- Regular updates to maintain accuracy
- Open source code available for peer review
