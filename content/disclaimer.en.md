### NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE

This application is NOT a medical device and is NOT intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of any disease or medical condition.

### Educational Purpose Only

- This tool is designed for educational and informational purposes only
- Results should be interpreted by qualified healthcare professionals
- Growth patterns require clinical context for proper evaluation
- Individual variations are normal and expected

### When to Consult Your Pediatrician

Consult your healthcare provider if you notice:

- Crossing multiple percentile lines (up or down)
- Sudden changes in growth patterns
- Growth consistently below 3rd percentile
- Growth consistently above 97th percentile
- Any concerns about your child's development

### Understanding Growth Charts

- Percentiles represent statistical distribution, not health judgments
- Most healthy children follow their individual growth curve
- Genetic factors significantly influence growth patterns
- Nutrition, health status, and environment affect growth
- Premature infants require adjusted age calculations

### Limitations of This Tool

- Does not account for prematurity adjustments
- Cannot assess growth velocity over time
- Does not include clinical context or family history
- May not be appropriate for children with certain medical conditions
- Does not replace professional pediatric assessment
